Election guidelines

01 · Verify your identity

Log in with your registration number and password. Each account maps to exactly one student record.

02 · Review every position

Read each candidate's manifesto and leadership record before marking your ballot.

03 · Vote once, confirm once

You may select one candidate per position. Votes are final once confirmed and cannot be edited.
